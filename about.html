Get to Know Me

Personal Information

My name is Kent Nathaniel Suñer Bienes, but you can simply call me Kent. I was born and raised in Iligan City. I consider myself an active and versatile person who can easily adjust to different situations and environments.

My Educational Journey

My educational journey began at Holy Family Progressive School, where I completed my elementary education. I continued my junior high school education under the Science Curriculum and later took the STEM strand at Iligan City National High School. Currently, I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Nursing at Mindanao State University - Iligan Institute of Technology.

I chose nursing because I see it as a profession where I can find purpose in life. For me, it is fulfilling to have a career where I can earn a living while serving others, helping patients and bringing hope to them and their families. Nursing also offers many opportunities for growth and different career paths.

My journey as a nursing student has been rocky and challenging. However, when I look back at everything I have achieved from first year up until today, I can say that I have made it this far. I am now in my final push toward graduation and becoming a registered nurse.

My Most Memorable Experience

One of my most memorable experiences was my Cebu exposure at Vicente Sotto Memorial Medical Center. It gave me the opportunity to experience quality healthcare and boosted my confidence during hospital rotations. I learned a lot from being exposed to different wards and gained valuable clinical experience.

Beyond our hospital duties, I also treasure the friendships I formed with fellow nursing students. We explored Cebu after duty and spent a month staying at a hotel, where we had time to relax and enjoy ourselves after exhausting hospital rotations. It was a meaningful experience both inside and outside the hospital.

My Personality and Interests

I am an active person who enjoys exploring and discovering new opportunities. I like engaging in activities that help me improve myself rather than spending the whole day in my room. I enjoy trying new things, stepping outside my comfort zone, and making the most of the opportunities around me.

Outside nursing, I enjoy volunteering, playing online games, basketball, jogging and pickleball. During my free time, I usually play pickleball or catch up on sleep especially after a long and tiring day. I particularly enjoy new experiences because they challenge me to push my boundaries and grow as a person.

A Glimpse of My Life

My Growth and Achievements

My entire nursing journey has been a collective experience that helped me mature and find my purpose in life. Every challenge, experience, and lesson has contributed to the person I am today.

I am proud of myself for continuing to try even when things do not go as planned. Whenever I fail, I take it as a learning experience and use it as motivation to keep moving forward. I believe I am only getting started, and I look forward to making my future self even prouder.

My Goals and Aspirations

As a nursing student, my goal is to become a competent and compassionate nurse who can bring smiles and hope to patients and their loved ones. I want to provide care that makes a meaningful difference in their lives.

After graduation, I hope to take some time to relax after more than two decades of studying. Of course, I also hope to pass the nursing licensure examination and, God willing, become a board topnotcher.

In the future, I aspire to be a just a chill person whom others can easily approach and befriend. At the same time, I want to remain driven to grow professionally, explore opportunities and continue improving myself.
